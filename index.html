Why bother

What a clogged console is actually doing to your gear

🌡️

Overheating

Dust-packed heatsinks trap heat around the CPU/GPU, forcing the console to throttle performance to stop itself cooking.

🔊

Fan noise

Fans spin harder to compensate for blocked airflow — that jet-engine sound is the console fighting its own dust.

⏳

Shortened lifespan

Sustained heat stress degrades thermal paste and solder joints over time — the leading cause of early console death.

💷

Cheaper than replacing

A clean costs a fraction of a repair or a new console, and takes a day instead of weeks.
